## Scoring and publication

O2 §§1.3–1.4: raw points are `correct − 0.25 × incorrect`; blanks contribute zero. Official normalisation adds a section-specific difficulty coefficient derived from actual CISIA cohort responses. Itatest lacks those data and must not label its own accuracy or raw points as a CISIA normalised result. University weighting and admission thresholds are institution-specific.

## Registration rules and unresolved boundaries

O5 confirms one attempt per macro-period and booking the next attempt from the day after the existing test. The current overview quotes EUR55 and card/MAV payment. O6 §3 describes account activation, format/university/date selection, payment and receipt confirmation; the receipt is the admission evidence. Its weekday deadline table closes at 14:00: Monday→previous Tuesday, Tuesday→previous Wednesday, Wednesday→previous Thursday, Thursday/Friday→previous Friday, with the stated public-holiday adjustment. Prefer the actual O8 session deadline when displaying a date.

O6 also documents booking changes subject to open bookings, seats and completed payment, and a one-use absence credit; its credit-expiry language references the old cycle. **Unresolved:** whether a revised PDF changes these detailed exceptions for 2026–27. Do not transplant old credit endpoints, identification times or old result-release dates into a new calendar without reconfirmation. O9 distinguishes exam results from university applications/rankings; no universal pass mark is established.

No full pre-amendment syllabus snapshot was obtained. Therefore only the added section and format/calendar changes are confirmed as changes; this audit does not assert that every old subject's detailed syllabus remained unchanged.
